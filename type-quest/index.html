How to Play ⌨️

1
Take turns

Pick who's playing. A banner shows whose turn it is, in their colour.

2
Type what you see

Tap the glowing key (or use a real keyboard). Each key is coloured for the finger that presses it.

🧠
Learn the word

Every word shows and says its meaning — so you learn new words as you type. Tap 🔊 to hear it again.

⭐
Earn stars

Neat, accurate typing earns more stars. Wrong keys just wobble — never a buzzer!

📊
Grown-ups

The Parents page (with a PIN) shows each child's words learned, accuracy and speed, per theme.

Settings ⚙️

No data is collected and the game never goes online. Everything stays on this device. Private & safe. 💛
