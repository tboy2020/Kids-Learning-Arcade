How to Play 🔺

1
Take turns

Pass the device around. A banner shows whose turn it is, in their color.

2
Find it

Tap the shape or color the game asks for.

🔁
What comes next?

Look at the pattern, then tap the piece that comes next in the row.

💡
Stuck? Hint!

Tap 💡 for help. Wrong taps just wobble and say "try again" — never a buzzer!
