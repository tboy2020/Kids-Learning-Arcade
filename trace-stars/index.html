How to Play ✏️

1
Take turns

Pass the device around. A banner shows whose turn it is, in their color.

2
Start on the green dot

Put your finger on the green dot, then slide along the path.

✏️
Light up all the dots

Touch every dot to trace the letter, number or shape. Tap 👀 Show me if you need help.

⭐
Finish & shine!

Light them all and your letter sparkles — you win stars! Tap ↩️ to try again anytime.

Settings ⚙️

No data is collected and the game never goes online. Private & safe. 💛
