How to Play 🎵

1
Take turns

Pass the device around. A banner shows whose turn it is, in their color.

🔁
Repeat the tune

Watch and listen to the pads light up, then tap them in the same order!

🎶
Play a song

The next pad glows — tap it to play a real song like Twinkle Twinkle.

👂
High or low

Listen to two notes and pick which one was higher. Tap 🔊 to hear again!
