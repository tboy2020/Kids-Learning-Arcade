How to Play 🃏

1
Take turns

On your turn, flip two cards to find a matching pair.

2
Make a match

Cards match when they belong together — like A & a, 3 & three dots, or cat & 🐱.

⭐
Match = keep going!

Find a pair and you score a point and get another turn. No match? The cards flip back and it's the next player's turn.

🧠
Remember!

Watch where the cards are. The player with the most pairs wins!

Settings ⚙️
