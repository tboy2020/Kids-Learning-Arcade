How to Play 🫧

1
Take turns

Pass the device around. A banner shows whose turn it is, in their color.

2
Listen

Tap 🔊 and listen carefully to the letter, sound or word.

👂
Pop the bubble

Tap the bubble that matches — the right letter, the starting/ending sound, or the word that rhymes.

💡
Stuck? Hint!

Tap 💡 for help. Wrong taps just wobble and say "try again" — never a buzzer. Learning is fun here!

Settings ⚙️

No data is collected and the game never goes online. Private & safe. 💛
